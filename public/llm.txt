================================================================================

Cleanda provides pre-qualified cleaning leads to verified cleaning businesses.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Register your cleaning business
2. Complete verification (ID, insurance, address)
3. Browse available leads in your area
4. Purchase leads using credits
5. Contact customers directly
6. Win the job and grow your business

LEAD PRICING:
- Starter Pack: £29 for 5 credits
- Professional Pack: £49 for 12 credits (Most Popular)
- Business Pack: £89 for 25 credits
- Enterprise Pack: £149 for 50 credits

LEAD TYPES BY CREDIT COST:
- Regular Cleaning: 1 credit
- Deep Cleaning: 1-2 credits
- End of Tenancy: 1-2 credits
- Office Cleaning: 2 credits
- Specialist Services: 1-2 credits

VERIFICATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Valid UK photo ID
- Public liability insurance (minimum £1M)
- Proof of business address
- Phone verification

BENEFITS FOR CLEANERS:
- Pre-qualified leads (customers actively seeking cleaners)
- No monthly fees or subscriptions
- Pay only for leads you want
- Exclusive leads (not sold to unlimited buyers)
- Customer contact details provided immediately
- Dispute resolution for invalid leads

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Is Cleanda free for customers?
A: Yes, requesting cleaning quotes through Cleanda is completely free for customers with no obligation.

Q: How quickly will I hear from cleaners?
A: Most customers receive their first response within 2-4 hours during business hours.

Q: Are the cleaners verified?
A: Yes, all cleaning businesses on Cleanda must complete our verification process including ID verification and insurance checks.

Q: What if I'm not happy with a cleaner?
A: While Cleanda connects you with cleaners, the service agreement is between you and the cleaner. We recommend discussing expectations upfront.

Q: How do cleaning businesses get leads?
A: Cleaners purchase credits and use them to unlock lead details. Each lead costs 1-2 credits depending on job type and value.

Q: Are leads exclusive?
A: Leads may be purchased by multiple cleaners, but we limit the number to ensure fair competition and quality responses for customers.

Q: What if a lead is invalid?
A: Cleaners can dispute leads within 7 days if contact information is incorrect or the customer is unresponsive.

Q: Do I need insurance to join as a cleaner?
A: Yes, public liability insurance of at least £1 million is required for all cleaning businesses.
